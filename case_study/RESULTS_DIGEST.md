## 2b. Mixed outcomes within a stay

A per-stay (trace-global) verdict has to assign one outcome label to the whole stay. This section quantifies how often that label would necessarily be wrong for part of the stay: stays containing both a failed and a successful breathing trial.

**Numbers for prose**

- 7,994 of 9,698 stays with two or more evaluable episodes have mixed outcomes (82.4%) — 67.8% of all 11,790 multi-episode stays, 22.4% of the whole 35,692-stay cohort.
- In 82.4% of multi-outcome stays a global verdict would assign one label to episodes whose true outcomes disagree.
- Even collapsing each mixed stay to its majority outcome (the best case for a trace-global label) mislabels 10,008 episodes — 33.7% of the episodes inside mixed stays, 18.2% of all evaluable episodes in the cohort.
- Mixed stays contain a median of 1 failed trials (mean 2.34).

## 2c. What segmentation buys (trace-global ablation)

Same clinical model, same stays, evaluated a second time with segmentation disabled (`main_per_sbt.py --no-segmentation`): one evaluation trace per stay instead of one per trigger episode. Comparing the two verdicts on the same 2,000 multi-episode stays isolates what episode-local evaluation adds, holding the checker, the model, and the population fixed. This ablation covers the multi-episode subpopulation only — it shows where segmentation *can* matter, not an average over the whole cohort.

**Numbers for prose**

- 24,866 stay-level violations at episode level collapse to 25,880 at trace level across 20 comparable constraints (1 excluded as not comparable — see below).
- 434 stays are flagged violated at episode level but not at trace level: an overall masking rate of 1.7% — a violation in one trial discharged or hidden once all trials are pooled into a single trace.
- 1,745 of 2,000 stays disagree between the two levels on at least one constraint.
- Mean activation loss under pooling: 47.2% across comparable constraints — activations from separate trials merge into a single per-stay activation, which is also why vacuity rates drop at trace level.
- Excluded as not comparable: INT-COMPOSITE-01 (activates only for attempt_index > 1, which never occurs under a single whole-stay trace).

## 4. Outcome association

Fisher's exact test per constraint on the 2x2 table of **exposure = constraint violated** against **outcome = success**. OR < 1 means violation tracks failure (the constraint discriminates); OR > 1 means violation tracks success. Rules with `outcome_scope: next_episode` are scored against the *next* episode's outcome, which is why their sample is smaller.

**Numbers for prose**

- 21 constraints tested; 17 significant at raw p < 0.05, 17 after Benjamini-Hochberg FDR, 16 after Bonferroni (family size = 21).
- 13 constraints have OR < 1 (violation tracks failure), 8 have OR > 1.
- Clinical-relevance classification: useful 10, harmful 7, inconclusive 4.
- 1 estimates are degenerate (zero cell, continuity correction, or |log OR| beyond the separation threshold) and are excluded from the headline table.
- Reproduction check against the committed `statistical_results_summary_both.csv`: **PASS** (21 rows, cells/OR/p identical).
